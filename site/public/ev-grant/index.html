How much E-Auto grant do you get?

Germany pays 1,500 to 6,000 € towards a new electric car or plug-in hybrid registered since 1 January 2026, bought or leased. The amount depends on your household income and children, not on the car's price. Your numbers stay on this device.

    How to get it

    1. Buy or lease the new car first. It must be first registered in your name on or after 1 January 2026. The purchase date does not matter. Used cars do not qualify.
    2. Apply within 12 months of registration, online at the Förderzentrale Deutschland (run by BAFA). You log in with BundID, using your ID card's online function or an ELSTER certificate.
    3. Have ready: the vehicle identification number (FIN), your last two income tax assessments (not older than three years), proof of child benefit for the child bonus, and for a plug-in hybrid its certificate of conformity.
    4. Keep the car for 36 months from first registration. Each person can get the grant once, and a household can apply at most twice.

    Questions: BAFA hotline +49 6196 908-1009 or elektromobilitaet@bafa.bund.de. The budget is 3 billion € for about 800,000 cars until 2029.

    Rules as of 9 November 2026, and where they come from

    From the federal environment ministry's questions and answers and the ADAC summary. Grant = base for the income tier (electric 5,000 / 4,000 / 3,000 €, plug-in hybrid 3,500 / 2,500 / 1,500 € up to 45,000 / 60,000 / 80,000 €) plus 500 € per child under 18, up to two. The income limit of 80,000 € rises by 5,000 € per child. Fuel-cell cars get the electric rates. Plug-in hybrids and range extenders count when first registered by 30 June 2027.

    Plug-in hybrid figures in the picker are official WLTP averages over the versions registered in Germany, from the EEA CO2 monitoring of new cars (CC BY 4.0). Your exact version can differ: its certificate of conformity decides.

    An estimate, not a decision. BAFA decides on your application under the funding guideline in force.
