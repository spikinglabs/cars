How this is calculated, and where the numbers come from

Formula: cost per 100 km = kWh/100 km × (1 + losses) × €/kWh. Break-even = L/100 km × €/L ÷ (kWh/100 km × (1 + losses)).

Fuel cars: the most registered new models in Germany in 2025 (KBA) plus the Mazda CX-30 and CX-5, each with its most common engine. Consumption is a real-world average rounded from owner logs on spritmonitor.de, higher than the WLTP figure. EV figures are rough real-world estimates. Official consumption is the WLTP figure at the plug reported for every new car registered in Germany, averaged over the versions people bought, from the EEA CO2 monitoring of new cars (CC BY 4.0). For cars without an estimate, the four driving levels are 0.9, 1, 1.22 and 1.38 times that figure.

Energy only: purchase price, tax, insurance and servicing are not included. Your tariff, the weather and how you drive change the real cost.
