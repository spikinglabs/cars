Formula: cost per 100 km = kWh/100 km × (1 + losses) × €/kWh. Break-even = L/100 km × €/L ÷ (kWh/100 km × (1 + losses)).

Fuel cars: the most registered new models in Germany in 2025 (KBA) plus the Mazda CX-30 and CX-5, each with its most common engine. Consumption is a real-world average rounded from owner logs on spritmonitor.de, higher than the WLTP figure. EV figures are rough real-world estimates.

Energy only: purchase price, tax, insurance and servicing are not included. Your tariff, the weather and how you drive change the real cost.
