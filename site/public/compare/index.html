    Often compared

    The most registered models in Germany, in pairs.

    Official consumption and CO2 are the EEA type-approval (WLTP) averages over the versions of a model registered in its newest year; a range shows when versions differ. Real driving usually uses more. Electric cost uses home charging at €0.35/kWh plus 10% losses; plug-in hybrids use their official electric and fuel mix. Registrations count the KBA model with every drive type together. Recalls are EU Safety Gate alerts since 2019 that name the model; whether a car is affected depends on its VIN.
