Privacy notice (Datenschutzerklärung)

Who is responsible

The controller under the GDPR for this website is SL Spiking Labs UG (haftungsbeschränkt), Subbelrather Str. 15 a, 50823 Köln, Deutschland, hi@spikinglabs.com.

Hosting

The site runs on Cloudflare Pages (Cloudflare, Inc., USA; certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, with a data processing agreement). To deliver pages, Cloudflare necessarily processes your IP address and technical request data and keeps short-lived security logs. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, our interest in a secure, working website.

No account, no cookies, no tracking

Allo Cars has no accounts and sets no cookies. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics. To see which parts of the site are used, each page view and a few taps (such as downloading a data file or installing the app) add one to a daily total per country in our own usage counter (monitoring.admin.allo.land, a Cloudflare Worker run by us). It keeps only the day, the site, the name of the page section or action, and your country, and for page views also the kind of device and browser and the network operator's name (to spot automated visits): no IP address, no ID, no cookie, nothing that could identify you, and nothing is stored on your device. It is skipped if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, our interest in knowing which parts of the site are used. What you type into a tool stays in your browser and in the page address, so it is only shared if you share the link.

Stored on your device

Some choices are kept in your browser's local storage so the site remembers them next time: light or dark, your country, your language, and the last settings of the charging cost calculator (car, driving style, prices and compared cars). On phones we also count there whether you have seen or closed the hint about installing the app, so it stops showing. A service worker keeps copies of the pages and data you opened, so they open quickly and work offline. All of this stays on your device and is never sent to us. It is needed for features you use (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can remove it by clearing the site data in your browser.

Fonts and data files

All fonts, scripts and data files load from this site. No font service or other third party receives your data when you open a page, with one exception: the map on the charger map page, described next.

Charger map

The charger map loads its map images (tiles) from the tile servers of the OpenStreetMap Foundation (St John’s Innovation Centre, Cambridge, United Kingdom; the UK has an EU adequacy decision). Your browser sends them your IP address and the part of the map you look at. This only happens on the charger map page. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, our interest in showing a map without running our own map server. See the OSMF privacy policy. If you press "Near me", your browser asks for your location; it is used only in your browser to centre the map and is never sent to us; the tile servers only see which tiles of that area load.

Open in Claude

If you press "Open in Claude", your browser opens claude.ai with your tool inputs and results in the message box. Anthropic (USA, EU-US Data Privacy Framework) then receives them under its own privacy policy. Nothing is sent unless you press the button. "Copy for AI" only copies text to your clipboard.

Connector for AI assistants

If you add the Allo Cars connector (cars.allo.land/mcp) to an AI assistant, the assistant sends us what it looks up, such as a car model or a town. We answer from public data. To see which tools are used and to fix errors, we keep a record of each request in our own Cloudflare R2 storage (Cloudflare, Inc., as above): the time, the tool and what it was asked for (car models and towns as sent; positions rounded to about 10 km and postcodes cut to their first two digits; e-mail addresses, vehicle identification numbers and long numbers removed), the name and version of the assistant app, the first part of its user-agent header, the country Cloudflare derives from the request, how long the answer took and whether it failed. We do not store your IP address, any ID, cookie or the answer, so the record cannot be linked to you. It is not published, and we keep it as long as it helps us improve the connector. Each connection, tool call and error also adds one to a daily total per country in our usage counter described above. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, our interest in knowing how the connector is used and keeping it working. Cloudflare handles the request as for every page. Your assistant's provider decides what it stores about your chat.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Email us to use them. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority.
