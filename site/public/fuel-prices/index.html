How the forecast works

Every week we score three simple forecasters on the last two years of this series, as if we had run them each week back then: no change (the last price carried forward), trend (the average weekly change of the past year) and momentum (an autoregression on the weekly changes). The one with the smallest average error over weeks 1 to 8 is shown, but only if it beats "no change" by at least 2 %; otherwise the forecast is "no change".

The bands come from the chosen forecaster's own past errors: 8 in 10 of its past forecasts that many weeks ahead fell inside the darker band, 19 in 20 inside the lighter one. No distribution is assumed. The approach follows research on naive forecasting by the Allo Cars maker (extrapolation-as-forecasts): a forecast is worth only what it adds over "no change".

A forecast cannot know the news. Oil shocks, tax changes or a weak euro move prices faster than any of these methods can see.
