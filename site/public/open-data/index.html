Data and sources

Where Allo Cars gets its numbers, what you can download, and how an AI assistant can use the tools.

Charging prices

Public ad-hoc charging prices

Every hour we read the published ad-hoc tariffs (pay without a contract) of public chargers in Germany from the Open ChargePoint Database run by MobiData BW. It merges the AFIR price feeds that operators publish on Mobilithek. We keep only sources with an open licence, and we keep every collection, so the archive grows by itself. No open archive of earlier prices exists, so our series starts on 6 Nov 2026.

Free to download, no key and no sign-up:

  • /data/charging-prices/latest.json: today's median, 10th and 90th percentile, overall and per source
  • /data/charging-prices/daily.csv: one row per day and source
  • /data/charging-prices/tariffs.csv: every current ad-hoc tariff with energy, time, parking and session prices
  • /data/charging-prices/sources.json: sources and their licences

Prices are the base energy price of each ad-hoc tariff. Session and blocking fees are in tariffs.csv. Contract prices are usually lower.

When you reuse the data, credit the sources and licences listed in sources.json.

Electricity prices

Day-ahead electricity prices

Several times each afternoon we read the day-ahead prices of the German power exchange (bidding zone DE-LU) from SMARD, the energy market portal of the Bundesnetzagentur. Since October 2025 they come in 15-minute steps; tomorrow's prices are published around 13:00. Our history goes back to October 2018.

The price you pay on a dynamic tariff is the exchange price plus a fixed add-on (grid fee, levies, electricity tax, supplier margin) plus 19 % VAT. We assume 0.18 €/kWh before VAT; enter yours on the page.

  • /data/electricity-prices/de/latest.json: today and tomorrow in 15-minute steps, with the cheapest 1 to 8 hour windows
  • /data/electricity-prices/de/daily.csv: one row per day since 2018, in €/MWh
  • /data/electricity-prices/de/hourly.csv: every hour since 2018, in €/MWh

Source: Bundesnetzagentur | SMARD.de, licence CC BY 4.0.

Recalls

Car recalls

Every day we read the weekly reports of the EU Safety Gate and keep every alert in the category Motor vehicles since 2019: cars, motorcycles, vans, trucks and e-scooters. Germany's Kraftfahrt-Bundesamt (KBA) reports each German vehicle recall there, as do the other EU countries, Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein and Northern Ireland. For the models in our tools that are also sold in the US, we add the recall campaigns of the US National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA).

Free to download, no key and no sign-up:

  • /data/recalls/eu/latest.json: counts and the newest alerts
  • /data/recalls/eu/models.json and /data/recalls/eu/models/<model>.json: alerts per followed model, for example volvo-ex30
  • /data/recalls/eu/brands.json and /data/recalls/eu/brand/<brand>.json: alerts per brand
  • /data/recalls/eu/recalls.csv: every alert since 2019, one row each
  • /data/recalls/us/models/<model>.json: NHTSA campaigns for the same models

Safety Gate data: © European Union, reused under Commission Decision 2011/833/EU. NHTSA data is a US government work in the public domain. Whether a particular car is affected depends on its VIN.

Registrations

New cars by model

The Kraftfahrt-Bundesamt (KBA), Germany's federal motor transport authority, publishes how many passenger cars of each brand and model series were newly registered every month (statistic FZ10). We read each monthly table once it comes out, about a week after the month ends, and keep every month since December 2018.

Free to download, no key and no sign-up:

  • /data/registrations/de/latest.json: the newest month: all new cars by drive type, the top 20 models and the top 10 electric models, with the change on a year before
  • /data/registrations/de/models.json: every model ever listed, with its file, newest count, rank and last 12 months
  • /data/registrations/de/models/<slug>.json: one model per month, for example volvo-ex30.json
  • /data/registrations/de/monthly.csv: every model, every month (month, brand, model, slug, total, diesel, hybrid, phev, bev)
  • /data/registrations/de/market.csv: all new cars per month by drive type and the electric share
  • /data/registrations/de/brands.json: every brand per month, total and electric

Counts are new registrations in the month. hybrid includes plug-in hybrids, which have their own phev column since 2021. bev is battery electric only. Model names are as the KBA writes them; SONSTIGE means a brand's other models.

Source: © Kraftfahrt-Bundesamt, Flensburg, under the Data licence Germany, attribution, version 2.0. When you reuse the data, credit the KBA the same way.

Fuel prices

Petrol, diesel and LPG prices

Every week the European Commission publishes national average pump prices, including all taxes, in the Weekly Oil Bulletin (CC BY 4.0). We keep the full series since 2005 for every EU country and the EU average,. The chart is on the fuel price page.

  • /data/fuel-prices/weekly.csv: one row per week since 2005, € per litre
  • /data/fuel-prices/latest.json: this week, the change on last week, 4- and 52-week averages
  • Other countries: /data/fuel-prices/<country>/…, for example eu or at; the list is in index.json
Calculators

Car consumption figures

The EV charging cost tool starts each car with rough real-world consumption for four ways of driving: efficient, typical, motorway at 120 km/h, and winter. They are our estimates for comparison, not manufacturer figures. Change them to what your trip computer shows. The formula is open: kWh per 100 km × (1 + charging losses) × price per kWh. The code is in /ev-cost/calc.js.

For AI assistants

Use Allo Cars from an AI

  • Copy for AI. Every tool has a button that copies your inputs, the results and the link as plain text you can paste into any assistant.
  • Links that carry the inputs. A tool's settings live in its address, for example /ev-cost/?car=Volvo EX30&p=0.35. An assistant can build such a link to open the tool already filled in.
  • Machine-readable lists. /tools.json describes each tool and its parameters, and /llms.txt lists the pages and data files.

A connector that lets Claude run the tools directly is planned. Each page is also in German under /de/, with the same link parameters.

Privacy

What we keep about you

Nothing. There is no account, no cookie and no tracking. Your inputs stay in the page and its link. Your browser only stores your light or dark choice and whether you closed the install hint. Details are in the privacy notice.
