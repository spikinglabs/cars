    Buying an electric car in Germany

    Five questions in the order most buyers ask them, each answered with today's numbers. Every answer opens the full tool with your inputs.

    1. How much grant do I get?

      1,500 to 6,000 € for a new car registered since 1 January 2026, by household income and children.
